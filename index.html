Your Past Doesn't Get the Final Say

For anyone who's been impacted by the justice system, or held back by stigma, guilt, or fear, this is for you if you're ready to stop letting your past define what comes next.

The Weight That Keeps You Stuck

A record follows you into every job application, even when you're qualified for the role. The guilt from choices you already paid for doesn't stay in the past; it shows up every time someone looks at you differently. Stigma tells you your interest in something new, like tech or AI, isn't for people like you. Imposter syndrome convinces you that everyone else got a head start you'll never catch up to. It's easy to stay stuck there, believing there's no point in trying.

I Know Because I Lived It

I served time. I came home qualified for jobs that rejected me anyway because of a record I'd already paid for. I know what it's like to battle substance abuse and mental health, to feel hopeless, to wonder what the point was when the options in front of me felt so limited. There was a point where I believed my interest in tech, in building things, in learning something new, wasn't meant for someone like me. I was wrong. I made the choice to change, and I enrolled in a program built for exactly this kind of transition. If you're reading this and you recognize yourself in it, you're not stuck. You're just not moving yet.
